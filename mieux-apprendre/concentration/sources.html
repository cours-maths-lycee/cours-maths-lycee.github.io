Sources de l'atelier

Références vérifiées en octobre 2026, dans l'ordre de l'atelier.

Une concentration mise à l'épreuve

  • Mark, G. (2023). Attention Span: A Groundbreaking Way to Restore Balance, Happiness and Productivity. Hanover Square Press. Entretien (université de Californie)
  • OCDE (2024). Students, digital devices and success, d'après PISA 2022 ; OCDE (2023). Résultats du PISA 2022 (Volume II). Temps d'apprentissage et climat disciplinaire

Les deux formes d'attention et leur développement

  • Corbetta, M. & Shulman, G. L. (2002). Control of goal-directed and stimulus-driven attention in the brain. Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 3, 201-215.
  • Fortenbaugh, F. C. et al. (2015). Sustained attention across the life span in a sample of 10,000: dissociating ability and strategy. Psychological Science, 26(9), 1497-1510. Présentation par l'APS
  • Slattery, E. J. et al. (2022). Popular interventions to enhance sustained attention in children and adolescents: a critical systematic review. Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 137. Notice
  • Sur le mythe des « 8 secondes » : origine du chiffre (rapport marketing de 2015).

Une attention limitée et sélective

  • Simons, D. J. & Chabris, C. F. (1999). Gorillas in our midst: sustained inattentional blindness for dynamic events. Perception, 28, 1059-1074.
  • Simons, D. J. (2010). Monkeying around with the gorillas in our midst. i-Perception, 1, 3-6.
  • Lennie, P. (2003). The cost of cortical computation. Current Biology, 13, 493-497. PDF
  • Lachaux, J.-P. (2020). L'attention, ça s'apprend ! MDI. Vidéos du programme ATOLE.

L'attention et les écrans

  • Stothart, C., Mitchum, A. & Yehnert, C. (2015). The attentional cost of receiving a cell phone notification. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance, 41(4), 893-897. Présentation
  • Ward, A. F. et al. (2017). Brain drain: the mere presence of one's own smartphone reduces available cognitive capacity. Journal of the Association for Consumer Research, 2(2).
  • Hartmann, M., Martarelli, C. S., Reber, T. P. & Rothen, N. (2020). Does a smartphone on the desk drain our brain? No evidence of cognitive costs due to smartphone presence in a short-term and prospective memory task. Consciousness and Cognition, 86. Présentation par la BPS
  • Böttger, T., Poschik, M. & Zierer, K. (2023). Does the brain drain effect really exist? A meta-analysis. Behavioral Sciences, 13(9). Présentation
  • Delgado, P. et al. (2018). Don't throw away your printed books: a meta-analysis on the effects of reading media on reading comprehension. Educational Research Review, 25, 23-38. PDF
  • Clinton, V. (2019). Reading from paper compared to screens: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Journal of Research in Reading, 42(2), 288-325.
  • Nielsen Norman Group. F-shaped pattern for reading web content (2006, mis à jour en 2017).
  • Wiradhany, W. & Nieuwenstein, M. (2017). Cognitive control in media multitaskers: two replication studies and a meta-analysis. Attention, Perception, & Psychophysics, 79. Présentation

Le multitâche

  • Rubinstein, J. S., Meyer, D. E. & Evans, J. E. (2001). Executive control of cognitive processes in task switching. Journal of Experimental Psychology: HPP, 27(4), 763-797. Synthèse de l'APA
  • Monsell, S. (2003). Task switching. Trends in Cognitive Sciences, 7(3), 134-140.
  • Watson, J. M. & Strayer, D. L. (2010). Supertaskers: profiles in extraordinary multitasking ability. Psychonomic Bulletin & Review, 17(4), 479-485. Présentation
  • Strayer, D. L. & Drews, F. A. (2007). Cell-phone-induced driver distraction. Current Directions in Psychological Science, 16(3), 128-131.

Les plans « Si… alors… »

  • Gollwitzer, P. M. & Sheeran, P. (2006). Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 38, 69-119.
  • Duckworth, A. L., Grant, H., Loew, B., Oettingen, G. & Gollwitzer, P. M. (2011). Self-regulation strategies improve self-discipline in adolescents: benefits of mental contrasting and implementation intentions. Educational Psychology, 31(1), 17-26.

Durée de concentration, pauses et temps d'écran

  • Bradbury, N. A. (2016). Attention span during lectures: 8 seconds, 10 minutes, or more? Advances in Physiology Education, 40, 509-513.
  • Albulescu, P. et al. (2022). "Give me a break!" A systematic review and meta-analysis on the efficacy of micro-breaks for increasing well-being and performance. PLOS ONE, 17(8). Présentation
  • Arcep, Arcom, CGE, ANCT (2025). Baromètre du numérique, édition 2025.
  • Santé.fr, « Plus de 4 heures d'écran par jour pour les 6-17 ans » (cite Esteban, Santé publique France, 2015, et Ipsos pour le Centre national du livre, 2024).
  • Goodyear, V. A. et al. (2025). School phone policies and their association with mental wellbeing, phone use, and social media use (SMART Schools). The Lancet Regional Health – Europe. Présentation
